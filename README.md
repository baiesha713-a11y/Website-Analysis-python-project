# Website-Analysis-python-project

Website Traffic & Engagement Analysis (Python)
📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes website traffic, user behavior, and engagement metrics using Python.
The goal is to understand how users arrive at the website, how they engage with content, and which channels drive the most value, enabling data-driven marketing and content decisions.

🎯 Objectives

Analyze trends in users and sessions over time

Identify top-performing traffic acquisition channels

Measure user engagement across channels

Compare traffic volume vs engagement quality

Provide actionable business recommendations

📂 Dataset Description

The dataset contains hourly website analytics data with the following key fields:

Channel Group (Direct, Organic Social, Organic Search, Email, Referral, etc.)

Date & Hour

Users

Sessions

Engaged Sessions

Average Engagement Time per Session

Engagement Rate

Events per Session

Total Event Count

📁 Data Source: Website Analytics Export
📊 Granularity: Hour-level data

🛠 Tools & Technologies Used

Python

Pandas – Data manipulation

Matplotlib / Seaborn – Data visualization

Jupyter Notebook – Analysis environment

📈 Key Insights
🔹 Traffic Trends

Sessions and users follow a similar trend over time, showing a strong correlation between traffic and user growth.

Regular peaks and drops indicate time-based or campaign-driven traffic patterns.

Sudden spikes suggest short-term marketing or content-driven activities.

🔹 User Acquisition Channels

Organic Social is the largest user acquisition channel, outperforming all others.

Direct, Organic Search, and Referral channels contribute consistent secondary traffic.

Email, Organic Video, and Unassigned channels currently have minimal traffic impact.

🔹 Engagement Analysis

Organic Video shows the highest average engagement time, indicating strong content quality.

Referral and Email traffic demonstrate moderate engagement, suggesting trust-based visits.

Direct and Organic Search bring higher volume but relatively lower engagement per session.

📊 Sample Metrics Snapshot

Users and sessions peak during specific evening hours

Engagement rates remain close to 50%, indicating healthy interaction

Events per session remain consistently above 4 events, showing active user behavior

✅ Business Recommendations
📌 Traffic & Campaign Strategy

Launch major campaigns and content during peak traffic hours to maximize reach.

Use low-traffic periods for targeted promotions to stabilize user activity.

Analyze spike periods to replicate successful marketing strategies.

📌 Channel Optimization

Invest more in Organic Social content and campaigns to scale growth.

Optimize SEO and referral partnerships to strengthen mid-performing channels.

Reassess low-performing channels (Email, Video) and test new content formats.

📌 Engagement Improvement

Scale Organic Video efforts and repurpose high-performing videos across platforms.

Strengthen email and referral strategies with personalized, value-driven messaging.

Improve landing pages and content relevance for Direct and Search traffic to boost engagement.
